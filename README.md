## ▚ RESEARCH

**Adversarial Machine Learning under Drift: Comparative Evaluation of Adaptive Defence Mechanisms**
Master of Cybersecurity (thesis stream), La Trobe University

A drift detector cannot tell a real distribution shift from an injected one. So an adversary does not need to fool your model. They only need to fool the thing that decides when to retrain it, and let your own defence throw away a model that was still working.

<div align="center">
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/code-cyber-arch/code-cyber-arch/main/assets/drift.svg" width="100%" alt="Adversarial drift response policy" />
</div>

My contribution is a confirmation gate: do not adapt on the first alarm, adapt only once the change persists. Across 252 paired runs it cuts adaptation events by roughly 80% and improves accuracy, with no exceptions in the pairing. The detector never has to distinguish natural drift from adversarial drift, which is the part nobody has solved.

<details>
<summary><b>Experimental design</b></summary>

<br>

| | |
|---|---|
| **Base learner** | Hoeffding Tree, fixed, used as an instrument rather than a variable |
| **Detectors** | ADWIN, HDDM_W (error-based) · HDDDM, D3 (feature-based) |
| **Response policies** | none · immediate · confirmed (k=2) · two RL-gated arms |
| **Attacks** | instance-level label flipping and concept injection, L ∈ {0.05, 0.15, 0.25} |
| **Streams** | SEA_A, RBF_I (synthetic) · RADAR ransomware behaviour (real) |
| **Runs** | 567 |

## ▚ 4D BIM AND CONSTRUCTION SEQUENCING

Ten years in architecture and BIM, five of them building 4D construction sequences for Australian projects. A 4D model is a geometry model crossed with a programme: you watch the building assemble on the schedule it is actually going to be built on, and you find out in the model rather than on site that two trades are booked into the same space in week eleven.

<div align="center">
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/code-cyber-arch/code-cyber-arch/main/assets/bim-4d.svg" width="100%" alt="4D construction sequencing" />
</div>

<details>
<summary><b>What that work actually involves</b></summary>

<br>

Synchro 4D Pro for the sequencing, After Effects for the motion graphics that go into tender submissions, and a lot of scripted cleanup in between. Six years lecturing on this at the University of Gondar before moving to Australia.

The link back to the research is not decorative. A construction programme is a model of the future that starts being wrong the moment it is published, and the interesting question in both fields is the same one: how do you tell a signal that means *adapt now* from one that means *wait, this will pass*.

</details>
